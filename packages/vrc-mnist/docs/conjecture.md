# Falsifiable conjecture

With operator parameters and the 2×28×28 real-valued representation of a complex optical state fixed,
increasing recurrence time should improve held-out performance, with a larger benefit for harder inputs.
All evaluation checkpoints freeze the operator. Evidence must include nonlinear and one-pass controls,
a locally connected matched-budget feed-forward control, wrong→right and right→wrong transitions, and
unseen corruptions. Confidence growth without correction is not supporting evidence.
